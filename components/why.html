Why Pawbin

Toy shopping took twenty tabs. Now it's one shelf.

Every pet parent has that drawer: squeakers that died in a day, a £9 feather wand the cat looked at once, three “indestructible” chews in pieces by Tuesday. We buy the toys, we test them for four weeks with real cats and dogs, and we only ship the ones that survive.

No filler. No 400-page catalogue of identical tennis balls. Just the shelf we'd recommend to a friend.

Step 01

Vet-checked first

Non-toxic rubbers, food-grade silicone, AZO-free dyes. Anything with a loose squeaker never makes it past the desk.

Step 02

Four-week play trial

Caught live by 38 tester households: 22 dogs, 31 cats, and a spaniel who has destroyed £400 of “tough” toys.

Step 03

Chew-power rating

Every toy ships with a 1–5 chew rating, so you can pick for a lap-cat or a labrador without guessing.
