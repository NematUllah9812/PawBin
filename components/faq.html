Delivery & returns

Questions pet parents actually ask.

How fast is UK delivery — and what does it cost?

Orders placed before 1pm on a working day leave Sheffield the same day. Royal Mail Tracked 24 lands in 1–2 working days (from £3.49, free over £25) and we also offer DPD next-day for £5.99. Highlands, islands and Northern Ireland are covered at the same rates — we don't add a surcharge.

What if my dog destroys it in a week?

Our 60-day chew-proof promise covers chew damage, split seams and dead squeakers on anything rated 3/5 or higher. Send one photo through your account and we'll refund or replace it — no return postage, no arguing. It's how we learn which toys get pulled from the shelf.

Do you ship outside the UK?

We ship to the UK, Isle of Man and Channel Islands by default. EU and international shipping is available at checkout from £7.90, but customs fees are the buyer's responsibility. Trade customers inside the EU can be quoted DDP delivery on account.

Are the toys safe for kittens and puppies?

Yes. Every product page lists a chew rating and an age guide, and our kitten/puppy range uses no loose parts, no small bells and no plastic eyes. Food-grade silicone and AZO-free dyes are standard across the range, and anything with an electrical component is fully enclosed.

Can I buy wholesale or drop-ship Pawbin?

Yes — that's what our trade console is for. Approved accounts get trade pricing from £4.10 per unit, blind-branded dispatch, live stock sync to Shopify, WooCommerce, Etsy and eBay, and no minimum order. Open a trade account from the footer and we'll verify you within one working day.

How do returns work if my cat simply isn't interested?

If a toy has been ignored rather than chewed, you have 30 days to send it back for a full refund — we'll email a prepaid label. Unused items in original packaging are refunded within two working days of arriving back with us. Faulty on arrival? We replace first and sort the paperwork after.
